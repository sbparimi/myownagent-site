Questions

Before you ask.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Your agent lives in WhatsApp, which you already have. You text it like any other contact.

Will it send things without asking me?

No. Emails, applications, bookings, posts and anything else that leaves in your name wait for your OK. You can give a standing yes to routine jobs (say, one daily post) and take it back with one message.

Is my data private?

Your agent only connects to the accounts you choose, and watchers are read-only unless you ask for more. Your information is used to do your work and nothing else. Disconnect any account at any time and we stop.

What can't it do?

It won't lie for you — forms get truthful answers, and when there is no truthful option it stops and asks. It can't pass a CAPTCHA, sit your interview or sign contracts. Some sites block automated access; those steps come to you as a one-tap list. It never moves money without your explicit approval.

What do I need to give you?

A setup call, your goals and rules, your CV or brand basics, and access to the accounts you want it to work in. You stay the owner of every account.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. The setup is a one-time fee of €299 + VAT. There is no subscription to cancel.

How fast is it live?

You leave the setup call with your agent running. More areas are added step by step so you can check each one.
