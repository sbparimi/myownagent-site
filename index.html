Jobs done, not automations

12 jobs. Pick any 3.

Your setup includes the 3 jobs you pick from this list, and it does each one start to finish. Free tools move data from one place to another. These are whole jobs, with the judgment calls in the middle. Hand over the job, not the steps.

01

Job applications, start to finish

Suitable roles found every day, a CV rewritten for each one, the forms filled in, every reply tracked.

Why not a free tool? An alert only lists jobs. Reading each ad, tailoring the CV truthfully and spotting the interview reply takes judgment, every time.

02

A live dashboard, built and kept up to date

One page with your numbers, applications, leads or orders, fed from your mail and files and refreshed on its own.

Why not a free tool? A template gives you empty boxes. Deciding what goes on it and pulling the data out of messy emails is the job.

03

Your website, system or tool, watched

Checked around the clock. When something breaks or changes, you get an alert saying what went wrong and a fix drafted.

Why not a free tool? An uptime checker says “down”. It doesn't look at what broke, work out why or write the fix.

04

Supplier quotes hunted and compared

Quotes from several suppliers, compared on price, delivery and conditions, with a clear pick and the reason.

Why not a free tool? Comparison sites show their own partners. Real quotes come from emails, calls and PDFs, and someone has to read the small print.

05

Calls to businesses in Dutch

Calls to shops and local businesses for quotes, bookings, order status and follow-ups, with a short summary of what was agreed.

Why not a free tool? Free tools don't pick up the phone, handle the questions or the “can you call back tomorrow”.

06

Posts, ads and short videos made

Content made from your products, photos and news, in your style, and scheduled once you approve it.

Why not a free tool? A scheduler only posts what you already made. Here the idea, the words, the visuals and the edit are done.

07

A website built and published

A working site with your text, photos and contact details on your own web address, plus changes whenever you ask.

Why not a free tool? A website builder hands you a blank template and a weekend of work. Here you get a finished site.

08

Anything stuck, chased until it moves

Unpaid invoices, missing replies and late orders followed up politely, step by step. You see each message before it goes out.

Why not a free tool? A reminder app nudges you. Chasing means reading each reply, choosing the next step and knowing when to call.

09

Research reports

A short, sourced report on a market, a competitor, a supplier or a decision you face, with a recommendation at the end.

Why not a free tool? Search gives you links. A report means reading the pages, checking the facts and saying what to do.

10

Inbox handled, replies in your voice

Every morning what needs you is on top, the rest is sorted, and replies are already written the way you write.

Why not a free tool? Filters sort by sender. Knowing what matters to you, and answering like you would, needs judgment.

11

New leads found and followed up

Prospects found and researched, personal first emails written, every reply spotted and an answer drafted for your OK.

Why not a free tool? Mailing tools blast one template. Picking the right people and writing to each one properly is the real work.

12

Renewals and contracts watched

A heads-up before a subscription or contract renews, with the cancel-by date, and the cancellation done on your OK.

Why not a free tool? A calendar reminder needs you to know the date first. This reads it from your mail and does the cancelling too.

Questions

Before you ask.

How is this different from free tools?

Free tools move data from one place to another, and you still do the work. Your Personal Assistant does the whole job: it reads, decides, writes, calls and follows up, and checks with you before anything goes to your customers.

Do I need to install anything?

No. You hand over jobs in WhatsApp, which you already have. You text it like any other contact.

Will it send things without asking me?

No. Emails, applications, bookings, posts and anything else that leaves in your name wait for your OK. You can give a standing yes to routine jobs (say, one daily post) and take it back with one message.

Is my data private?

Your Personal Assistant only connects to the accounts you choose, and watchers are read-only unless you ask for more. Your information is used to do your work and nothing else. Disconnect any account at any time and we stop.

What can't it do?

It won't lie for you — forms get truthful answers, and when there is no truthful option it stops and asks. It can't pass a CAPTCHA, sit your interview or sign contracts. Some sites block automated access; those steps come to you as a one-tap list. It never moves money without your explicit approval.

What do I need to give you?

A setup call, the 3 jobs you want done, your rules, your CV or brand basics, and access to the accounts it should work in. You stay the owner of every account.

What does it cost?

€49.99 + VAT one-time setup for any 3 jobs, then €5.99/month + VAT. The monthly fee keeps your jobs running, fixes them when a site or form changes, and covers marketing help: posts, ads and short videos, drafted for your OK. Need more than 3 jobs? Ask us when you reserve.

Can I get a refund?

No refunds or returns once your first job has been delivered. Questions about a payment? Contact us and we reply by email.

What do I get for €5.99 a month?

Maintenance and marketing. Your 3 jobs keep running every day, and when a website, form or tool changes we fix the job so it doesn't stop. On top of that you get marketing help each month: posts, ads or short videos, drafted for your OK before anything goes out.

How fast is it live?

You leave the setup call with your 3 jobs running. Each one is checked with you step by step.
