Questions

Before you ask.

Why does the agent carry my name?

Because it's yours. It is built for one person, learns your rules and your voice, and works only on your accounts. It is “Maya’s agent”, not a shared app — and it signs off the way you tell it to.

Is my data private?

Your agent only connects to the accounts you choose, and watchers are read-only unless you ask for more. Your information is used to do your work and nothing else. Disconnect any account at any time and we stop.

Will it send things without asking me?

No. Emails, posts, applications, bookings and anything else that leaves in your name follow the rules you set in the build. By default every first-time action waits for your OK; you can give a standing yes to routine ones (say, one daily post) and take it back with one message.

What can't it do?

It won't lie for you — forms get truthful answers, and when there is no truthful option it stops and asks. It can't pass a CAPTCHA, sit your interview or sign contracts. Some sites block automated access; those steps land on your tap list. It doesn't move money without your explicit approval.

What do I need to give you?

A discovery call, your goals and rules, your CV or brand basics, and access to the accounts you want it to work in. You stay the owner of every account.

Can I change or stop it?

Yes. Text a change and it is applied; say “stop” and that routine stops. The setup is a one-time fee; there is no subscription to cancel.

How fast is it live?

The first routine usually runs within days of the discovery call; more areas are added step by step so you can check each one.
